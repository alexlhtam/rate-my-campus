# What Rate Our Campus does

A student-made map of UCL. Find places to study and live, read honest reviews, and see what's happening on campus right now.

## Campus map

- **3D or flat.** Pan, zoom and rotate. UCL buildings are shaded purple and stand up in 3D.
- **Jump to a campus.** Bloomsbury, UCL East, or all UCL sites across London.
- **Building directory.** Search by name, nickname or address. Filter by campus.
- **Layers.** Show or hide places, events, freebies, food, deals and incidents.
- **Crowded spots.** Several pins in one place open as a list.
- **Older devices.** A simpler flat map still works if the 3D one can't load.

## Finding places

- **190 places to review.** All 181 buildings in UCL's official directory, plus nine libraries and rooms inside them.
- **Five types.** Study spaces, lecture halls, student halls, campus buildings, rentals.
- **Plain-English search.** Try "a quiet study spot with plugs".
- **Filters.** Place type, distance, dietary preference, open now.
- **Distances.** From main campus, any building, or your location (only if you ask; it stays on your device).
- **Opening hours.** Real published times only. Two places so far (Student Centre, Senate House Library); everywhere else says "unknown" rather than guess.
- **Before you visit.** Official arrival notes for the Main, Science, SSEES and IOE libraries.

## Campus assistant

Ask a question; Claude, an AI assistant, finds the answer.

- **Any question.** "Where is the archaeology library?" or "Two quiet study spaces near Bloomsbury?"
- **Any language.** Ask in your language, get the answer in that language.
- **Sources included.** Every answer links to where the information came from.
- **On the map.** Answers appear as temporary pins, just for you.
- **Beyond campus.** Looks up museums, cafés and other places through Google Maps.
- **Honest.** Says when it can't confirm something. Never invents a location.
- Requires sign-in.

## For international students

New campus, new city, maybe a new language. The app helps with all three.

- 🗣️ **Ask in your own language.** The assistant answers in the language you ask in. (The rest of the app's screens are in English for now.)
- 🏷️ **Nicknames work.** Search finds buildings by the names people actually use.
- 🧭 **Plan your first trip.** Campus views, distances from any building, official library arrival notes.
- 🏠 **Choose where to live.** Halls and rental reviews cover what's hard to judge from abroad: deposits, damp, landlords.
- 🥗 **Food that fits.** Filter free food for vegan, vegetarian, halal or gluten-free.
- 🤝 **Meet people.** Ask questions on place pages; find societies through freebies and events.
- ☎️ **Know who to call.** 999 and UCL Security numbers are on every incident screen.
- 👀 **Look first.** Demo mode works before you have a UCL email.

## Reviews

- **Out of five, plus "would you recommend it?"**
- **Scores that fit the place:**

  | Place type | Rated on |
  | --- | --- |
  | Study spaces, lecture halls | Comfort, plug sockets, Wi-Fi, quietness, accessibility |
  | Campus buildings | Comfort, Wi-Fi, accessibility |
  | Student halls | Value, cleanliness, social life, staff, location |
  | Rentals | Landlord responsiveness, deposit returned, damp or mould, value, noise |

- **Tags and a written review**, with the term and year you were there.
- **At-a-glance summary.** Average rating, % who recommend, score averages, top tags.
- **Sort and filter.** Most recent or most helpful; filter by year.
- **Helpful votes** on other people's reviews.
- **One review per place.** Edit or delete it any time.
- **Anonymous.** Your name and email are never shown.

## Discussion

- **Ask any place a question.** Other students reply.
- **Follow places** to hear about new reviews and discussions.
- **Inbox.** New activity on places you follow and replies to you. In-app only, no emails or phone notifications.
- **Community tab.** Recent conversations from across campus.

## Free food

- **Available now.** Food you can collect, with a countdown.
- **Search and sort.** By food, place, diet, nearest, or ending soon.
- **Full details.** Where, how to get in, how much is left, ingredients and allergens, directions.
- **"Still here" or "Gone".** Students keep each other updated; enough "gone" closes the post.
- **First come, first served.** No booking or payment.
- **Share in three steps.** Describe it, confirm details, pin it. Claude can draft it from your message or a label.
- **Safety first.** Allergen info comes from the sharer. "Unknown" never means allergen-free.
- **Manage your posts.** Update amounts, change instructions, close when it's gone.

## Freebies, events and deals

- **Society freebies.** Free merch, snacks and giveaways.
- **Student deals.** Original price, deal price and how much you save.
- **Post a find.** Add a photo; Claude drafts the details, or type them yourself.
- **What's on.** Events in a list and on the map. Filter: today, now, this week, two weeks.
- **Real freebies only.** Free entry, raffles and things for sale don't count.
- **⏸ Union events import: Not live yet.** Fills the map with the next two weeks of Students' Union UCL events. Off until the Union confirms we can use its listings.
- **⏸ Daily auto-refresh: Not live yet.** Built but off. Needs the Union import first.

## Campus incidents

Live heads-ups for fights, spills, fire alarms, medical emergencies, security issues, protests and disruption.

- **Red pins and banner.** Urgent ones pulse. The Alerts tab shows a live count.
- **Report in two steps.** What, how serious, where, how long (30 min to 24 h). Photo optional.
- **"I can see this too" / "It's over".** Three or more "over" votes that outnumber confirmations end it.
- **No duplicates.** Same type nearby? You're sent to the existing alert.
- **Short-lived.** Every alert ends within 24 hours. Cleared alerts stay visible for three hours.
- **Not an emergency service.** The app contacts nobody. 999 and UCL Security are shown first on every screen.
- **What, not who.** Reports are checked so they don't identify anyone.

## Saving and sharing

- **Save places** to the Saved tab (stored in your browser).
- **Share** any place or alert by link or downloadable QR code.

## Your account

- **UCL email sign-up.** @ucl.ac.uk only. Needed to post, review or vote; anyone can browse.
- **My contributions.** See what's live, waiting for a check, or hidden, and why.
- **Export your data** or **delete your account** any time.
- **Note:** sign-up doesn't yet send a confirmation email to prove the inbox is yours.

## Safety and moderation

- **Automatic checks.** Claude checks every review, post, photo and incident. Suitable ones go live straight away; doubtful ones wait for a moderator.
- **Report button everywhere.** Enough reports hide content until a moderator looks.
- **Moderator queue** for held content, reports and requests.
- **Photo privacy.** Photos are resized and stripped of hidden details like location.
- **Takedown requests.** Anyone can ask, no account needed.
- **Map corrections.** Spot a mistake? Suggest a fix; a moderator checks it.
- **Auto clean-up.** Expired alerts, unused photos and closed requests are deleted on a schedule.
- **Community guidelines.** First-hand, fair, no private names or contact details.

## Demo mode

- **Fictional sample content**, clearly labelled.
- **Nothing leaves your browser.** Demo posts never reach real students.
- **Switch to real activity** any time.
- **Local Wi-Fi sharing.** Open the demo on other devices on the same network for a showcase.
