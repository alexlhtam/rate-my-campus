# What Rate Our Campus does

Rate Our Campus is a map of UCL made by students, for students. It puts places to study, live and hang out on one map, next to what's happening around campus right now: free food, society freebies, events, student deals and safety alerts. You can read honest reviews from other students, ask questions and share what you find.

This page walks through every feature in plain language. Anything that's built but not switched on yet is marked **Not live yet**.

---

## The campus map

The map is the heart of the app. Everything else shows up on it.

- **Move around freely.** Pan, zoom and rotate the map, and switch between a flat view and a 3D view where UCL buildings stand up off the map.
- **Jump to a campus.** One tap takes you to Bloomsbury, UCL East, or a view of all UCL locations across London.
- **See which buildings are UCL's.** UCL buildings are shaded purple. Tap one to see its name, what it is and where the information came from.
- **Find a building by name.** The building directory searches by name, nickname or address, and you can filter it by campus. Buildings we haven't been able to place on the map are still listed and still searchable, with a clear note saying their location isn't mapped yet.
- **Turn layers on and off.** Show or hide rated places, events, freebies, free food, deals and incidents so the map only shows what you care about.
- **No pin gets lost.** When several things sit at the same spot, tapping it opens a list so you can pick each one.
- **Works on older devices.** If your browser can't draw the full map, you get a simpler flat map that still shows pins and lets you move around.

## Finding places

- **Every UCL building, ready to review.** All 181 buildings in UCL's official directory have their own page, plus separate pages for nine libraries and rooms inside them. That's 190 real places in total.
- **Place types.** Study spaces, lecture halls, student halls, campus buildings and rentals.
- **Search in your own words.** Type something like "a quiet study spot with plugs" and the app filters places to match.
- **Filters.** Narrow results by place type, distance, dietary preference (for food), or what's open now.
- **Distances from where you want.** Measure distance from UCL's main campus, from any building you choose, or from your own location. Your location is only used if you ask, and it stays on your device.
- **Opening hours.** "Open now" and "open late" use real published opening times. Right now only two places have confirmed hours (the Student Centre and Senate House Library). Everywhere else shows hours as unknown rather than guessing.
- **Getting there.** Four libraries (Main, Science, SSEES and IOE) have short "before you visit" notes taken from UCL's official pages, such as which floor the entrance is on and where the lifts are.
- **Buildings and the rooms inside them.** A library page links to the building it's in, and a building page lists the rooms inside it. Their ratings are kept separate.

## The campus assistant

Ask a question and Claude, an AI assistant, finds an answer for you.

- **Ask anything about campus.** For example, "Where is the archaeology library?" or "Find two quiet study spaces near Bloomsbury and explain the trade-offs."
- **Answers you can check.** The assistant searches the app's own list of places and researches the web. Its answers come with links to the sources it used.
- **Shows it on the map.** Places in the answer appear as temporary pins. They're just for you and disappear when you search again.
- **Places outside the catalogue.** It can look up somewhere not on our list, such as a museum or café, through Google Maps.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** If it can't confirm something, such as whether a place has plug sockets, it says so instead of making it up. It never invents a location.
- You need to be signed in to use the assistant.

## For international students

Arriving at UCL from abroad means learning a new campus, a new city and often a new language all at once. Rate Our Campus is built to make that easier.

- **Ask in your own language.** The campus assistant understands questions in any language Claude supports and answers in the same one. You can ask "我可以在哪里安静地学习？" or "¿Dónde está la biblioteca de arqueología?" and get a sourced answer and map pins, the same as asking in English. The rest of the app's screens are in English for now.
- **Learn campus names quickly.** UCL buildings often go by nicknames and abbreviations. The building directory and search match nicknames and addresses as well as official names, so you can find a place however you heard it described.
- **Find your way before you go.** Map views for Bloomsbury and UCL East, distances from any building you choose, and official "before you visit" notes for four libraries help you plan a trip to somewhere you've never been.
- **Choose where to live with real experiences.** Student halls are rated on value, cleanliness, social life, staff and location. Rentals are rated on landlord responsiveness, getting your deposit back, and damp or mould: things that are hard to judge from another country.
- **Food that fits your diet.** Free food can be filtered for vegan, vegetarian, halal and gluten-free, with a reminder to check allergens with the person sharing.
- **Meet the community.** Ask a question on any place's page and get answers from students who've been there. Society freebies and Union events help you discover groups to join.
- **Know who to call.** Every incident screen shows the UK emergency number (999) and UCL Security's numbers, which are easy to miss when you're new.
- **Look around before you sign up.** Demo mode lets you explore the whole app with sample content before you have a UCL email address.

## Reviews and ratings

- **Rate a place out of five** and say whether you'd recommend it.
- **Scores that fit the place.** Each type of place is rated on what actually matters:
  - Study spaces and lecture halls: comfort, plug sockets, Wi-Fi, quietness, accessibility
  - Campus buildings: comfort, Wi-Fi, accessibility
  - Student halls: value, cleanliness, social life, staff, location
  - Rentals: landlord responsiveness, deposit returned, damp or mould, value, noise
- **Quick tags and a written review.** Pick a few words that fit and add your experience in your own words, along with the term and year you were there.
- **Summaries at a glance.** Each place shows its average rating, the percentage of people who'd recommend it, an average for each score and the tags people use most.
- **Sort and filter reviews.** View the most recent or most helpful reviews, and filter by year.
- **Helpful votes.** Mark reviews as helpful or not helpful.
- **One review per person per place.** You can update or delete your review at any time.
- **Anonymous to others.** Your name and email are never shown next to your reviews.

## Community discussion

- **Ask a place a question.** Every place has a discussion area where you can start a conversation ("Is it busy on Monday mornings?") and other students can reply.
- **Follow places you care about.** Follow a place to get its new reviews and discussions in your inbox.
- **Your campus inbox.** One place to see new activity on places you follow and replies to your conversations. This is inside the app only. You won't get emails or phone notifications.
- **The Community tab** gathers recent conversations from across campus.

## Free food

Leftover food from events shouldn't go to waste.

- **See what's available now.** Browse free food you can collect right now, with a countdown to when collection ends.
- **Search and sort.** Search by food or place, filter by dietary preference, show only food ending in the next ten minutes, and sort by nearest or ending soonest.
- **Full details before you go.** Each post shows where to collect it, how to get in, roughly how much there is, ingredient and allergen information, and directions.
- **"Still here" or "Gone".** Students let each other know if food is still available. Enough "gone" reports close the post automatically.
- **First come, first served.** There's no booking, reserving or payment.
- **Share leftover food in three steps:** describe it and add a photo, confirm the details, then pin the collection point on the map. Claude can read your message or a food label and fill in a draft for you to check.
- **Safety reminders.** Allergen and dietary information comes from the person sharing. "Unknown" never means allergen-free, and the app reminds you to check before you eat.
- **Manage your posts.** Update the amount left, change instructions, or close the post when the food runs out.

## Society freebies, events and deals

- **Society freebies.** Spot free merch, snacks and other giveaways from societies around campus.
- **Student deals.** Share and find discounts around campus. Deal posts show the original price, the deal price and how much you save.
- **Post a find.** Add a photo and a short description, and Claude can fill in the details for you to check. Or type everything yourself. Then set how long it's available and drop a pin.
- **What's on.** Union events and freebies appear in a list and on the map. You can filter them to today, happening now, this week or the next two weeks. Each event shows its time, venue, organising society and a link to the original listing.
- **Freebies are clearly separated from events.** An event only counts as a freebie if its listing says something is actually given away. Free entry, raffle prizes and items for sale don't count.
- **Union events import: Not live yet.** The app can read the Students' Union UCL calendar for the next two weeks and use Claude to turn the listings into events and freebies on the map. A moderator can also import a single event from its link. This stays switched off until the Union confirms we're allowed to use its listings, so no Union events are on the map yet.
- **Automatic daily import: Not live yet.** A once-a-day refresh of Union events is set up but switched off. It only works once the Union import above is switched on.

## Campus incidents

A heads-up system for things happening on campus, such as a fight, a chemical spill, a fire alarm, a medical emergency, a security issue, a protest or a disruption.

- **Live alerts.** Active incidents show as red pins on the map. Urgent ones pulse. A red banner appears when there's anything live, and the Alerts tab shows how many.
- **Report in two steps.** Choose what's happening, how serious it is, a short headline, where it is and how long the alert should last (30 minutes to 24 hours). You can add a photo.
- **"I can see this too" or "It's over".** Other students confirm or clear an alert. If at least three people say it's over, and they outnumber those confirming it, the alert ends.
- **No duplicates.** If someone has already reported the same kind of incident nearby, you're pointed to their alert instead.
- **Alerts don't hang around.** Every alert ends within 24 hours. The person who reported it can mark it over or delete it. Recently cleared alerts stay visible for three hours.
- **Not an emergency service.** The app never contacts anyone. Every incident screen shows 999 and UCL Security's numbers first, and you have to confirm you understand this before you can report.
- **Describe what's happening, not who.** Reports are checked to make sure they don't identify anyone. They're never labelled as verified.

## Saving and sharing

- **Save places.** Bookmark places to find them again in the Saved tab. Saved places stay in your browser.
- **Share a link or QR code.** Every place and alert has a share button that copies a link or makes a QR code image you can download. If someone opens a link to an alert that has ended, they're told it's no longer available.

## Your account

- **Sign up with your UCL email.** Only @ucl.ac.uk addresses can create an account. You need an account to post, review or vote, but anyone can browse.
- **My contributions.** See everything you've posted and whether it's live, waiting for a check or hidden, with the reason.
- **Export your data.** Download a copy of everything you've contributed.
- **Delete your account.** Remove your account and your contributions permanently.
- **Your email isn't checked yet.** At the moment, sign-up checks that you typed a UCL address but doesn't send a confirmation email to prove the inbox is yours.

## Keeping it safe and fair

- **Automatic checks.** Claude checks every review, post, photo and incident before it goes public. Suitable posts are published straight away. Anything doubtful stays private until a moderator looks at it.
- **Report button everywhere.** Every review, discussion, post and incident can be reported. Enough reports hide something until a moderator reviews it.
- **Moderator tools.** Moderators have a queue to approve or reject held content, deal with reports and handle requests.
- **Photo privacy.** Photos are resized on your device, and hidden details like camera location are stripped out before they're stored.
- **Privacy and takedown requests.** Anyone, even without an account, can ask for content to be reviewed or removed.
- **Suggest a correction.** Signed-in students can report a mistake on the map, such as a wrong building location. A moderator checks it before anything changes.
- **Old content is cleared out.** Expired alerts, unused photos and closed requests are deleted after a set time.
- **Community guidelines.** Clear rules on what to post: first-hand experiences, no private names or contact details, and be kind.

## Try it without an account (demo mode)

- **A clearly labelled demo.** Explore the app with fictional sample reviews, alerts, food posts, discussions and incidents.
- **Try everything safely.** Anything you post in the demo stays in your browser and never reaches real students.
- **Switch to real activity** at any time to see only genuine posts from the community.
- **Share the demo on local Wi-Fi.** The demo can be opened on other phones and laptops on the same network for a quick showcase.

## Not built yet

These are ideas we've deliberately held back on, usually because there's no reliable information to base them on yet:

- **How busy a place is or how many seats are free.** There's no live data source for this yet.
- **A full map of entrances, facilities and rooms.** No complete, verified dataset exists, so we haven't guessed.
- **Map shapes for every building.** Some buildings are listed but not yet drawn on the map, because we only add shapes we can back up with a source.
- **A real directory of rentals and landlords.** The one rental on the map is a clearly marked fictional example.
- **Verified society accounts.** There's no way yet to prove someone runs a society.
- **Email or push notifications.** Updates appear only in the in-app inbox for now.
- **Rating trends over time.** There aren't enough real reviews yet to show meaningful trends.
