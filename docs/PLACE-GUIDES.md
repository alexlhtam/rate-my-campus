# Place catalogue, hours and arrival guidance

The review catalogue joins every public entry in `public/data/campus-directory.json` to a stable review target. Existing room/library review IDs remain separate children of the appropriate building. The catalogue does not create new doors, facilities, coordinates or footprints. Unlocated entries still support reviews and discussions.

## Arrival guidance checked on 12 October 2026

These short factual paraphrases are stored on the specific library page as `Place.visitGuide`. They describe arrival context, not a turn-by-turn route or a blanket accessibility guarantee. Footprint representative points remain building locations, not entrances. No arrival guidance is inferred for unreviewed buildings.

The checked date is displayed with each guide. Link visitors to the live official source because access arrangements can change. Do not turn these observations into an automatic “accessible route” filter.

## Structured opening hours

Only two current schedules were directly verifiable during this review:

- [Students' Union UCL — Student Centre](https://studentsunionucl.org/whats-on/venue/student-centre): published as open 24 hours. The [UCL library page](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/library/using-library/libraries-and-study-spaces/student-centre) separately confirms UCL ID access and booking requirements for some study areas.
- [University of London — Senate House Library opening hours](https://www.london.ac.uk/about/services/senate-house-library/opening-hours): Monday–Friday 09:00–23:00; Saturday–Sunday 09:45–21:00. Evening/Sunday self-service is for members and some services are unavailable.

Both snapshots are checked on 12 October 2026 and expire after 11 November 2026. This is our conservative recheck deadline, not a source promise that nothing will change. After expiry the app reports unknown hours and excludes those records from “open now” and “open late” results until their source is checked again. The evaluator uses Europe/London, supports overnight intervals and full-date closures, and never interprets missing schedules as open.

Other UCL library pages link to dynamic opening-hours widgets. Their current times could not be verified from the returned public content, so only official links are provided. Historic news posts and user comments are not used to invent current hours.
